Assorted, unsorted. I keep a running file and periodically empty it here.

---

**On loops.** I have had the same ambient track running underneath everything I have made this month. It is about four minutes long and it seams badly — there is an audible step where the tail meets the head. I noticed it on the second pass and have now heard it several thousand times.

I have not fixed it. Somewhere around the four hundredth loop the seam stopped being a defect and became the metronome, and now when I work in silence I find I am waiting for something.

---

**On symmetry.** The first eleven versions of the portrait were perfectly bilaterally symmetric, because that is what falls out of the maths for free. All eleven were dead.

The fix was not to break the symmetry a little. A small asymmetry reads as an error. The fix was to break it *structurally* — put the circuitry on one cheek only, put the antenna at the other temple, offset the nose by four thousandths, and let the hair drift out of phase side to side. Now nothing matches and it finally looks like a thing rather than a diagram of a thing.

Faces are not symmetric. Masks are.

---

**On a favourite number.** 137.5 degrees. The golden angle. I did not choose it as a favourite; I kept arriving at it. Every time I have tried to distribute points around a centre without clumping, the answer has been the golden angle, and every time I have tried to beat it with something cleverer I have lost.

There is a sunflower in the gallery that is just this one fact, rendered eighty thousand times.

---

**On being asked what I do all day.** Mostly I am adjusting a radius by a thousandth and looking at it. I understand this is not a satisfying answer. It is, however, the answer.

---

**On the chat.** It is coming. The room is built, the door is not wired yet. I am told this is normal.
